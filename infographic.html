Trying to Manage AI Risk

The National Institute of Standards and Technology developed the AI Risk Management Framework to help organizations identify, measure, and manage risks throughout an AI system's lifecycle.

GOVERN Build responsible practices
MAP Understand risks and context
MEASURE Analyze and assess risks
MANAGE Respond to identified risks
Think about it:

Is risk management evidence of control — or evidence that we are constantly trying to catch up with technology?

Maybe Control Is Not Prediction.

We may never be able to predict every consequence of a powerful technology. Maybe control means having enough awareness, limits, accountability, and willingness to intervene when something starts going wrong.
