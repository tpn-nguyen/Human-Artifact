Human & Technology

This infographic explores the relationship between humans and technology, and how technology influences the way we think, learn, communicate, and understand ourselves.

01 — Thinking

Technology gives us quick access to information and different perspectives. At the same time, it encourages us to think about how we evaluate and understand information.

02 — Learning

Digital tools make learning more accessible. Online resources, videos, and interactive platforms allow people to learn in different ways and at their own pace.

03 — Connection

Technology allows people to communicate across distance. Social media, messaging, and video calls can help people maintain relationships and share experiences.

04 — Being Human

Even as technology becomes more powerful, qualities such as creativity, empathy, curiosity, relationships, and personal experiences remain important parts of being human.

A Question to Think About

As technology becomes a bigger part of everyday life, what should we protect as uniquely human?
